These fonts are embedded into the binary so rendering is identical on
Linux, Windows, and macOS.

  by Vercel. Licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org),
  which permits redistribution and bundling.

- **Ubuntu-R.ttf / Ubuntu-M.ttf** — Ubuntu Font Family, © Canonical Ltd.
  Licensed under the [Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0](https://ubuntu.com/legal/font-licence),
  which permits redistribution and bundling.
- **DejaVuSans.ttf** — DejaVu Fonts, based on Bitstream Vera Fonts
  (© Bitstream, Inc.) with public-domain additions.
  [License](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html) permits
  redistribution and bundling.
